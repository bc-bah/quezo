Made for the full journey

Everything a small team needs to run a considered assessment: simple to use, careful by design.

01

Build a living question bank

Organize, search, preview, and reuse single-answer multiple-choice and true/false questions. Keep revisions distinct and traceable.

02

Set the rules. Publish with care.

Compose from fixed question versions; configure points, attempts, timing, availability, ordering, and result release. Preview before publishing.

03

Protect every learner attempt

Assign to named learners. Support answer changes, visible save status, recovery, server-enforced deadlines, and retry-safe finalization.

04

Turn completion into insight

Auto-score objective responses, apply result-release rules, and review learner history, submissions, completion, and scores—with role-based access.

Prototype intent

The whole loop.
Deliberately focused.

Intended for one organization and low-stakes use: administrators, authors, and learners; assigned quizzes; objective questions; dependable attempts; automatic scoring; and permitted results.

Out of scope

Full LMS features, multiple organizations, guest attempts, written/manual grading, proctoring, and formal-exam or production-readiness claims.
